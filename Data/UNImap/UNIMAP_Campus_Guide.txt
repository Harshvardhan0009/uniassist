# Lovely Professional University (LPU) - Campus Map, Blocks & Site Plan Guide

## University Overview and Total Blocks
The Lovely Professional University (LPU) campus has numbered blocks ranging from Block 01 up to Block 58, along with lettered sub-blocks, residential hostel blocks, administrative offices, auditoriums, sports facilities, and central services.
